PRIVACY POLICY

for the mobile application “Noir 2048”

Effective date: 17.09.2026
Application version: 0.2.21

This Privacy Policy (the “Policy”) defines the procedures for processing information and personal data of users of the mobile application “Noir 2048” (the “Application”).

The personal data controller is developer SKRAY (the “Controller”).

Official contact channel: 2048noir@gmail.com

1. General Provisions

1.1. Noir 2048 is a mobile puzzle game for devices running Android.

1.2. In version 0.2.21, the Application does not require registration, does not provide user accounts and does not use its own server to store game progress.

1.3. Most game data is stored locally on the User’s device.

1.4. The Policy applies to information processed by the Controller in connection with the use of the Application, and to data processing through third-party services where that processing is carried out on behalf of the Controller or in connection with the use of the relevant features of the Application.

2. Information Processed

Depending on the features used, the Application may process:

  • the player name entered by the User;
  • a UUID generated by the Application to identify the local game profile;
  • game data: points, the best score, coins, achievements, statistics, selected and purchased skins and other game state parameters;
  • Application settings;
  • a consent flag for local daily reward notifications (without server-based delivery);
  • unfinished game data, where provided for by the particular build;
  • technical information necessary for the operation of the Application and third-party services;
  • information about update availability and download status requested from RuStore when a network connection is available (the game does not download APK files itself).

The User determines the content of the name they enter and should not include excessive personal information in it.

3. Purposes of Processing

Information may be used to:

  • ensure the operation of the Application;
  • save and display local game data;
  • operate the game profile;
  • maintain statistics and achievements;
  • operate the in-game economy;
  • provide advertising features;
  • ensure technical stability and security;
  • comply with legal requirements.

Only data necessary for these purposes and proportionate to them is processed.

4. Local Storage

4.1. In version 0.2.21, game progress is stored locally on the User’s device.

4.2. There is no cloud synchronisation or storage of game progress on the Application’s own server.

4.3. Uninstalling the Application or deleting its local data may result in permanent loss of game progress.

4.4. The Controller does not have a backup copy of local game progress and does not guarantee that it can be restored after the Application is uninstalled or its local data is cleared.

4.5. Deleting local data on the device does not automatically delete information that may have been independently processed by third-party services.

5. Advertising and Third-Party Services

5.1. The Application uses rewarded advertising from the third-party service Yandex.

5.2. The relevant advertising SDK is used to display advertisements and may process technical and other information in accordance with its settings, documentation and applicable rules.

5.3. The Controller does not control the full scope of information that the third-party advertising service may process independently.

5.4. When the User interacts with an advertisement, further processing of information may be carried out by the relevant advertiser or third-party service in accordance with their own documents.

5.5. Third-party services may have their own privacy policies and data processing terms. Their use is also governed by the relevant documents of those services.

6. Disclosure of Information

6.1. The Controller does not sell Users’ personal data.

6.2. Information may be provided to third parties where necessary for the operation of the features and third-party services used, where provided for by law, or where necessary to protect the Controller’s rights and legitimate interests.

6.3. When third-party services are used, relevant information may be processed directly by those services in accordance with their own documents.

7. Retention Periods

7.1. Local game data is stored on the User’s device until the User deletes it, uninstalls the Application, clears its data or another event occurs that deletes the local information.

7.2. The retention period for information that may be processed by third-party services is determined by those services in accordance with their own rules and applicable law.

7.3. The Controller stops processing and destroys personal data when the purposes of processing have been achieved or other lawful grounds arise, unless retention is required by law.

8. Protection of Information

8.1. The Controller takes necessary and sufficient organisational and technical measures to protect the personal data processed, in accordance with applicable law.

8.2. However, absolute information security cannot be guaranteed.

9. User Rights

To the extent provided for by applicable law, the User may:

  • obtain information about the processing of their personal data;
  • request correction of inaccurate data;
  • request deletion of data or cessation of processing where lawful grounds exist;
  • withdraw consent where processing is based on consent;
  • send enquiries to the Controller regarding personal data processing;
  • exercise other rights granted by law.

To exercise their rights, the User may contact:

2048noir@gmail.com

10. Minors

10.1. The Application is an entertainment puzzle game and does not contain elements of violence, killing or gambling.

10.2. The Controller does not aim to intentionally collect children’s personal data.

10.3. Where applicable law establishes special requirements for processing minors’ personal data, those requirements are complied with in the prescribed manner.

11. Changes to the Policy

11.1. The Controller may amend this Policy due to changes to the Application, the services used, the law or other circumstances.

11.2. A new version takes effect upon publication unless it states otherwise.

11.3. The current version of the Policy must be available to the User through the relevant official source.
